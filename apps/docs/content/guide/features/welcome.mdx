Welcome & Farewell posts a message in a channel of your choice when someone
joins your server and when someone leaves, can attach a generated image card
carrying the member's avatar, can send a new member a private direct
message, and can give every new member a set of roles the moment they
arrive.

## Prerequisites

- **The bot is in your server.** Everything here is driven by the bot
  noticing members joining and leaving, and the dashboard refuses every
  request for a server the bot has not joined.
- **Nothing is switched on until you switch it on.** A server that has never
  opened this page has Welcome Message, Welcome Image, Farewell Message,
  Farewell Image, and Welcome DM all off, no welcome or farewell channel
  chosen, both message styles set to Plain message, and no auto-roles. Until
  you enable something and pick a channel, joins and leaves pass silently.
- **You can open the dashboard for the server.** Access goes to the server
  owner and to members whose current Discord permissions in that server
  include **Administrator** or **Manage Server**. Anyone else is turned away.
- **The bot can post in the channel you pick.** The welcome and farewell
  messages are sent by the bot into the channel you select; if the channel
  no longer exists, is not a text channel, or the bot cannot send there, the
  join or leave produces nothing and nothing is reported back to you in the
  dashboard.
- **The bot's own role sits above every auto-role.** On join, any auto-role
  that is positioned at or above the bot's highest role is skipped silently,
  and so is a role that has been deleted. The remaining ones are still
  applied, so a partly-working auto-role list looks exactly like a working
  one.
- **If the permission system is switched on** for your server — configured on
  the **Permissions** page in the guild sidebar — then the **Welcome** group
  there carries three permissions: **View Config** (*View welcome/farewell
  config*), **Manage Config** (*Update welcome/farewell settings*), and
  **Test Messages** (*Send test welcome/farewell messages*). View Config is
  what loads the page's settings, generates the server-side image preview,
  and decides whether the **Welcome** entry appears in the guild sidebar at
  all; Manage Config is what lets **Save Changes** succeed and what lets you
  upload or remove a custom background; Test Messages is what lets **Send
  Test Message** succeed.

## Configuring welcomes and farewells

Open **Welcome** from the guild sidebar. The page is headed *Welcome &
Farewell* — *Configure welcome and farewell messages, images, and
auto-roles.* — and has six tabs: **Welcome**, **Welcome Image**,
**Farewell**, **Farewell Image**, **Welcome DM**, and **Auto-Role**.

Two buttons sit under the tabs and apply to the whole page:

- **Save Changes** writes every tab at once, not only the one you are
  looking at, and confirms with *Welcome configuration saved*. Nothing on
  any tab takes effect until you press it — leaving the page first discards
  your changes.
- **Send Test Message** is greyed out until the Welcome Message toggle is
  on. Pressing it checks your saved configuration and shows *Test message
  will be sent to* followed by your saved welcome channel written as
  `<#` and the channel's numeric ID `>` — the notification is plain text, so
  the ID is not turned into the channel's name. It does not post anything in
  Discord. To put a real test message in the channel, run `/welcome test` in
  your server instead.

### What members see

When someone joins, three things happen in order. Your auto-roles are
applied first. Then the bot posts in the channel set on the Welcome tab —
provided **Welcome Message** is on and a channel is chosen — using the
message style, text or embed, and image you configured. Last, it sends the
Welcome DM if that is on. Bots that join are given no auto-roles and no DM,
but they do get the channel message.

When someone leaves, the bot posts in the channel set on the Farewell tab,
provided **Farewell Message** is on and a channel is chosen. There is no
farewell DM and no farewell auto-role — the Welcome DM and Auto-Role tabs
apply to joins only.

If the image card fails to render, the message is still posted without it.

### The `/welcome` command

| Command | What it does | Permission required | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/welcome test` | Posts a test welcome message into your configured welcome channel. | Manage Server (`ManageGuild`) | 5s |

The reply is visible only to you. `/welcome test` always builds the **embed**
form of the welcome message from the fields on the Welcome tab's embed
builder and posts that — it ignores the Message style setting, the plain
Message text, and the welcome image, so it is a check on the embed and the
channel rather than a rehearsal of what a real join produces. It uses your
own account as the stand-in member, so `{user}` and the other variables
resolve to you.

It refuses before sending in three cases, each with a named reason: *Not
Configured* (Welcome Message is off, which is also what you get on a server
that has never saved this page), *No Channel* (no welcome channel is
chosen), and *Invalid Channel* (the configured channel no longer exists or
is not a text channel). A fourth reason, *Test Failed* — *Failed to send the
test welcome message. Please try again later.* — comes back when the
configuration passed all three checks but the send itself did not go
through, which is what you see when the bot cannot post in an otherwise
valid channel.

### Welcome tab and Farewell tab

These two tabs carry the same controls. The Welcome tab is headed *Welcome
Message* — *Send a custom embed when a new member joins.* — and the Farewell
tab *Farewell Message* — *Send a message when a member leaves the server.*

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| The toggle beside the heading | Turns that message on or off. Off means joins (or leaves) produce no channel message at all, whatever else is configured on the tab. |
| **Welcome Channel ID** / **Farewell Channel ID** | Despite the label, this is a picker, not a text field, and it lists standard text channels only — announcement, forum, stage, and voice channels are not offered. It also offers a "none" choice; with no channel chosen, nothing is posted even while the toggle is on. |
| **Message style** | **Plain message** — *The image is posted at full width with optional text above it.* — or **Embed** — *The image is shown inside an embed box.* The choice decides which editor appears below and which one the bot uses on a real join. |
| **Message text** (Plain style only) | Up to 2000 characters, the text posted on its own line. Supports the variables listed below. Whatever you write, the posted message can only ever ping the member who joined or left: an `@everyone`, `@here`, or role mention still appears in the message text, but notifies nobody. Leave it empty and Plain style posts the image alone. |

Choosing **Embed** replaces the text box with the **Embed Builder**:

| Field | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Up to 256 characters, shown as the embed's heading. |
| **Description** | Up to 4096 characters, the embed's body. The variable browser beside the label inserts a variable at the end of what you have typed. |
| **Color (hex)** | A hex value such as `#a3a6ff`, used for the embed's left-hand stripe. |
| **Footer** | Up to 2048 characters, shown in small text at the bottom. |
| **Thumbnail URL** | Up to 2048 characters, an image shown small in the top-right corner. `{user.avatar}` here puts the member's own avatar there. |
| **Image URL** | A full `https://` address for a large image inside the embed. Unlike Title, Description, Footer, and Thumbnail URL, this is a plain text box with no variable-aware editing — variables you type into it by hand are still substituted when the message is sent. |

A live Discord-style preview under the fields shows how the embed will read.
The embed always carries a timestamp, whether or not you fill anything in.

**The variables**, usable in the plain message text and in every embed
field:

| Variable | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `{user}` | A mention of the member, which pings them. |
| `{user.tag}` | Their Discord tag. |
| `{user.name}` | Their username. |
| `{user.id}` | Their numeric user ID. |
| `{user.avatar}` | A link to their avatar image. |
| `{server}` | Your server's name. |
| `{server.id}` | Your server's numeric ID. |
| `{server.icon}` | A link to your server's icon, or nothing if the server has none. |
| `{membercount}` | The member count, with thousands separators. |

Every occurrence of a variable is replaced, not only the first.

### Welcome Image tab and Farewell Image tab

*Generate a custom image card with the member's avatar when they join.* /
*Generate a custom farewell card when a member leaves.* The toggle beside
the heading turns the card on; while it is off the tab shows no controls,
only *Enable the toggle above to configure welcome images.* on the Welcome
Image tab and *Enable the toggle above to configure farewell images.* on the
Farewell Image tab.

The card's large text is the member's display name in your server. The
smaller line under it is whatever you type as the subtitle, with `{user}` and
`{user.name}` becoming their username, `{user.displayname}` their display
name, `{server}` your server's name, and `{membercount}` the member count.
These are the only variables the image understands — the embed's other
variables are drawn as literal text here.

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Live Preview** | Renders the card as you change settings, using your own avatar and name against a placeholder server called *Your Server* with 1,234 members. **Instant** renders it in your browser as you drag a slider; pressing the button switches to **Server**, which builds the same card with the same server-side renderer the bot uses on a real join — slower, but it is the renderer that will actually produce the card in Discord. **Refresh** re-renders the current settings. |
| **Template** | Six layouts, each card showing its own description: **Starter** (*Classic centered layout. Clean and universal — works for any server.*), **Horizon** (*Landscape split with avatar on left and text on right. Modern and balanced.*), **Neon** (*Cyberpunk-inspired with neon accents and glow effects. Built for gaming servers.*), **Elegant** (*Refined layout with thin borders and subtle dividers. Premium and sophisticated.*), **Minimal** (*Ultra-clean with small avatar and compact text. Lets the background shine.*), and **Aurora** (*Dreamy gradient effects with soft glow. Ethereal and atmospheric.*). The template sets the card's size and where the avatar and text sit. |
| **Background** → **Solid Color** | A single colour behind everything, chosen with the swatch or by typing a hex value. |
| **Background** → **Gradient** | Six built-in gradients: midnight, ocean, sunset, forest, nebula, and ember. |
| **Background** → **Custom Image** | **Upload Image** picks a file from your computer — *JPG, PNG, or WebP. Max 3 MB.* A file over 3 MB is refused before it is sent, and a file whose contents do not match its declared type is refused by the server. Once uploaded the tab shows a *Background set* badge and a **Remove** button that deletes the stored file and drops the card back to the solid colour. |
| **Overlay Darkening** | On by default at 50%. The **Opacity** slider, in steps of 5%, darkens the background so text stays readable over a busy image. At 0% the overlay does nothing. |
| **Avatar** → **Shape** | **Circle**, **Rounded Square**, or **Square**. |
| **Avatar** → **Border Width** | 0 to 12. Zero removes the ring around the avatar. |
| **Avatar** → **Border Color** | The ring's colour, by swatch or hex value. |
| **Avatar** → **Glow Effect** | Off by default. Switching it on reveals a colour picker for the glow. |
| **Title (Username)** → **Font**, **Color**, **Size** | The font list is the eight shipped with FluxCore — Inter, Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono, Poppins, Playfair Display, Outfit, Orbitron, and Bebas Neue. Size runs 16 to 56 in steps of 2. |
| **Subtitle (Custom Text)** → **Text** | The line under the name, up to 200 characters. The hint under the box lists `{user.name}`, `{server}`, and `{membercount}`. |
| **Subtitle (Custom Text)** → **Font**, **Color**, **Size** | Same font list; size runs 12 to 36 in steps of 1. |
| **Accent Color** | *Used for decorations, borders, and template accents.* — what it changes depends on the template you picked. |
| **Delivery Mode** | **With embed message** puts the card inside the embed, **Before embed message** posts the card as its own message first and the embed after it, and **Image only (no embed)** posts the card and no embed at all. This setting applies only when Message style is **Embed** — in Plain style the card is always posted at full width alongside your message text. |

Every colour box on these two tabs takes a six-digit hex value such as
`#1a1a2e`. A shorthand like `#abc` or a colour name is rejected when you
press Save Changes: a bad value on the Welcome Image tab is refused with
*Invalid welcome image config*, and one on the Farewell Image tab with
*Invalid farewell image config*. Because Save Changes writes the whole page
at once, either refusal blocks the other tabs' changes too.

### Welcome DM tab

*Send a direct message to new members. Will silently fail if DMs are
disabled.* The toggle turns it on; the tab has only an embed builder, with
the same fields and variables as above, so the DM is always an embed —
there is no plain-text or image option for it, and no channel to pick.

Bots that join are never sent one. If a member's direct messages are closed
to your server, nothing arrives and nothing is reported to you.

### Auto-Role tab

*Automatically assign roles to new members when they join. Bots are
excluded. The bot must have a role higher than the roles listed here.*

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Role IDs (comma-separated)** | Despite the label, this is a picker over your server's roles, and you can select several. Every selected role is given to each human member who joins, as one action, with the reason *Auto-role on join* recorded in your server's audit log. |

This tab has no toggle of its own: an empty list means no roles are given, a
non-empty one means they are. Roles are given on join only — clearing the
list does not take a role back from anyone who already has it.

## Related pages

**Permissions**, **Role Panels**, **Logs**, and **Security** each have their
own entry in the guild sidebar. Here is where each meets Welcome & Farewell.

- [Permissions](/guide/features/permissions) — where the View Config, Manage
  Config, and Test Messages permissions described in the Prerequisites are
  granted.
- [Role Panels](/guide/features/rolePanel) — the other way FluxCore hands out
  roles. Auto-roles are given automatically on join and are never
  self-service; role panels are the reverse.
- [Logging](/guide/features/logging) — a separate record of members joining
  and leaving, written to its own log channel. It is configured independently
  of this page: a join can be logged with no welcome message, or welcomed
  with no log entry.
- [Anti-Raid](/guide/features/antiraid) — the **Security** sidebar entry. Its
  checks run *before* the welcome does: a member caught by the account-age
  filter or a join-rate spike is actioned and gets no welcome message, no
  welcome DM, and no auto-roles.

[Leveling](/guide/features/leveling) is the other feature that reacts to a
member's first moments in your server: joining grants no XP by itself, and
auto-roles are unrelated to level reward roles.
