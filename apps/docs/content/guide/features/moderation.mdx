Moderation gives your team twelve slash commands for acting on members and
channels, and records each action as a numbered case that you can search,
edit, and delete from the dashboard.

## Prerequisites

- **The bot is in your server.** The dashboard refuses every moderation
  request for a server the bot has not joined.
- **You can open the dashboard for the server.** Access goes to the server
  owner and to members whose Discord permissions include **Administrator**
  or **Manage Server**. Anyone else is turned away with a permission error.
- **Each moderator holds the Discord permission their command requires.**
  The required permission is listed against every command below. FluxCore
  checks that permission again when the command runs, so a moderator who
  loses it stops being able to run the command.
- **The bot's own role holds the same permission and sits above the members
  it acts on.** Every command except `/case`, `/cases`, and `/note` stops
  with an error when the bot lacks the permission the command needs. On top
  of that, `/ban`, `/tempban`, and `/softban` refuse a member the bot cannot
  ban, and `/kick` refuses a member it cannot kick — which is what happens
  when the target's highest role sits at or above the bot's.
- **Your own role outranks the member you are acting on.** `/ban`,
  `/tempban`, `/softban`, `/kick`, and `/timeout` refuse a target whose
  highest role is equal to or above yours. They also refuse you as a target,
  and refuse the bot itself.
- **If the permission system is switched on** for your server — on the
  **Permissions** page in the sidebar, where the Permission System panel
  reads *Active — admins are restricted to their assigned permissions* —
  then reading cases in the dashboard needs the **View Cases** permission,
  editing or deleting them needs **Manage Cases**, and both *reading and*
  changing the two settings at the bottom of the page need **Manage
  Settings**. Without Manage Settings the settings panel still appears, but
  it cannot load your server's values and falls back to showing **DM on
  Punishment** off and **Mod Log Channel** set to *None* — whatever they are
  really set to. Treat what that panel shows as meaningless until you hold
  Manage Settings. While the Permission System panel reads *Inactive*, every
  admin with Manage Server has full access.

## Using the moderation commands

All twelve commands are Discord slash commands, run from a channel in your
server. Each has a per-moderator cooldown: after a moderator runs a command,
that same command is unavailable to them for the number of seconds listed,
and running it early replies with the remaining wait.

### Acting on a member

| Command | What it does | Permission required | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/ban` | Bans the member. | Ban Members | 5s |
| `/tempban` | Bans the member and lifts the ban when the duration runs out. | Ban Members | 5s |
| `/softban` | Bans and immediately unbans, which deletes the member's messages from the last 7 days and leaves no standing ban. | Ban Members | 5s |
| `/kick` | Removes the member from the server without banning them. | Kick Members | 5s |
| `/timeout` | Applies a Discord timeout for a duration you give. | Moderate Members | 5s |

Their options:

- `/ban` — **user** (required), **reason**, **delete-days** (0–7, how many
  days of the member's messages to delete).
- `/tempban` — **user** (required), **duration** (required; pick one of 1
  hour, 6 hours, 12 hours, 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days, 30 days),
  **reason**, **delete-days** (0–7). The bot checks for expired tempbans
  every 60 seconds and unbans the member on the first check after the
  duration has passed, so an unban can land up to a minute late.
- `/softban` — **user** (required), **reason**.
- `/kick` — **user** (required), **reason**.
- `/timeout` — **user** (required), **duration** (required), **reason**.
  A duration is one number and one unit, such as `10s`, `5m`, `2h`, `1d`, or
  `1w`. The maximum is 28 days; a longer duration is rejected, and so is a
  duration the bot cannot read.

Leaving **reason** empty records the case with the reason *No reason
provided*.

### Cleaning up messages

| Command | What it does | Permission required | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/clear` | Deletes the most recent messages in the current channel, optionally only one member's. | Manage Messages | 10s |
| `/purge` | Deletes recent messages that match a set of filters. | Manage Messages | 5s |

- `/clear` — **amount** (required, 1–100) and **user**. The amount counts
  the messages examined, so filtering by a user deletes only that user's
  messages out of that many. Messages older than 14 days are skipped, so the
  count in the reply can be lower than the amount you asked for. The reply
  is visible to you alone.
- `/purge` — **amount** (required, 1–100), **user**, **bots** (true deletes
  only messages posted by bots), **contains** (text that a message must
  contain, matched without regard to case), and **has** (Links, Attachments,
  or Embeds). Messages older than 14 days are skipped before the filters are
  applied, and the command deletes up to **amount** of what is left. When
  nothing matches, it replies that no messages matched the filters. The
  reply is visible to you alone.

Neither command records a case.

### Locking a channel

| Command | What it does | Permission required | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/lock` | Denies Send Messages to `@everyone` in a text channel. | Manage Channels | 5s |
| `/unlock` | Clears that denial, returning the channel to its inherited permissions. | Manage Channels | 5s |

`/lock` takes **channel** (a text channel; the current channel when you omit
it) and **reason**. `/unlock` takes **channel** only. `/unlock` removes the
`@everyone` Send Messages override rather than setting it to allow, so a
channel goes back to whatever its category and role permissions grant —
not to "everyone can post". Neither command records a case.

### Looking up history

| Command | What it does | Permission required | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/case` | Shows one case by its number. | Manage Messages | 3s |
| `/cases` | Lists every case recorded for one user. | Manage Messages | 3s |
| `/note` | Records a note about a user without punishing them. | Manage Messages | 3s |

- `/case` — **id** (required, 1 or higher). The reply shows the action,
  target, moderator, reason, whether the case is still active, when it was
  created, and — for timeouts and tempbans — the duration and expiry.
  A number with no matching case in this server replies *not found*.
- `/cases` — **user** (required). Results come 10 to a page, with Previous
  and Next buttons when there is more than one page. Only the moderator who
  ran the command can press them, and they stop responding two minutes after
  the reply is posted; run the command again to page further.
- `/note` — **user** (required) and **text** (required, up to 500
  characters). The note is stored as a case with the action *note*, and the
  confirmation is visible to you alone. The member is never messaged about
  a note.

### What members are told

When **DM on Punishment** is on, `/ban`, `/tempban`, `/softban`, `/kick`,
and `/timeout` send the member a direct message naming your server and the
action taken, with the reason, and with the duration for timeouts and
tempbans. The message is sent before the punishment is applied. If the
member has direct messages closed, the punishment still goes through and
nothing is reported to you.

### Case statistics

Three cards across the top. **Total Cases** counts every case matching your
current filters, across all pages. **Active Tempbans** and **Last 24h** count
only the cases on the page you are looking at — tempbans that have not yet
expired, and cases created in the past day — so both change as you page
through the table.

### Cases

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Filter by User ID…** | Narrows the table to cases whose target is that Discord user ID, and returns you to page 1. It matches an ID, not a username. |
| **All Actions** | Narrows the table to one action — Ban, Tempban, Kick, Timeout, Softban, Warn, or Note — and returns you to page 1. Choosing *All Actions* clears the filter. Picking **Warn** returns an empty table: warnings are stored separately and never become cases. |
| Table columns | ID, Action, Target, Moderator, Reason, Date, Actions. Target and Moderator are Discord user IDs. A case with no reason reads *No reason*. |
| Edit reason (pencil) | Opens *Edit Case #N Reason*, where you rewrite the reason in up to 500 characters. Saving changes what `/case` and `/cases` report for that case; nothing else about the case changes, and the member is not messaged. |
| Delete case (bin) | Asks for confirmation, then removes the case permanently. A deleted case stops appearing in the table and in `/case` and `/cases`. Deleting a tempban's case also cancels its scheduled unban — the case is what the bot watches — so the member stays banned until someone unbans them in Discord. |
| **Previous** / **Next** | Move through the cases 10 at a time. They appear only when there is more than one page. |

Six commands write into this table: `/ban`, `/tempban`, `/softban`, `/kick`,
`/timeout`, and `/note`. Warnings are kept in their own records and have
their own dashboard page — they never appear here, even though the action
filter offers *Warn*.

### Settings

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **DM on Punishment** | *Send a DM to users when they are punished (ban, kick, timeout).* Switching it off stops `/ban`, `/tempban`, `/softban`, `/kick`, and `/timeout` from messaging the member; the punishment and its case are unaffected. A server that has never opened these settings behaves as though the switch is on. |
| **Mod Log Channel** | Stores your choice of one text channel, or *None*. The choice is saved and shown again when the page reloads. No moderation case is posted to the channel you pick: nothing in the bot reads this setting today. To have the bot post messages into a channel when something happens in your server, configure the **Logs** page instead. |

Both settings save the moment you change them — there is no separate save
button on this section.
