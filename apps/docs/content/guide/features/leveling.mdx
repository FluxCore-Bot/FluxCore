Leveling gives every member an XP total that grows as they talk in your
server and sit in voice channels, turns that total into a level, announces
level-ups, hands out roles you attach to particular levels, and ranks
everyone on a leaderboard readable in Discord and in the dashboard.

## Prerequisites

- **The bot is in your server.** The dashboard refuses every leveling
  request for a server the bot has not joined.
- **Nothing has to be switched on first.** A server that has never opened
  the leveling page behaves as though Enable Leveling, Voice XP, and
  Level-up Announcements are all on, XP per Message is 15, XP Cooldown is
  60 seconds, and Voice XP per Minute is 5. Members start accumulating XP
  as soon as the bot is in the server.
- **You can open the dashboard for the server.** Access goes to the server
  owner and to members whose Discord permissions include **Administrator**
  or **Manage Server**. Anyone else is turned away with a permission error.
- **The bot can hand out the roles you set as rewards.** When the bot
  cannot add a reward role to a member, the grant is skipped — nothing is
  posted in Discord and nothing appears in the dashboard, so a reward that
  the bot has no authority over looks exactly like a reward nobody has
  earned yet.
- **If the permission system is switched on** for your server — on the
  **Permissions** page in the sidebar, where the Permission System panel
  reads *Active — admins are restricted to their assigned permissions.* —
  then each part of the Leveling page loads only for admins holding the
  matching permission: the Leaderboard tab and the **Ranked Members** card
  need **View Leaderboard**; the Settings, Exclusions, and Multipliers tabs
  need **Manage Settings** to read *and* to change anything; the Role
  Rewards tab and the **Role Rewards** card need **Manage Rewards**. The
  **Leveling** entry is shown only to admins holding View Leaderboard, and it
  disappears from two places at once: the guild sidebar, and the search box
  you open with ⌘K or Ctrl+K (⌘P and Ctrl+P open the same box). Hiding the
  entry does not close the page, though — an admin who reaches it by its
  address anyway, from a bookmark or a shared link, still gets the page and
  its five tabs rather than a refusal. What a
  missing permission actually produces is an empty tab, not a message
  saying you lack it: the Leaderboard tab reads *No members have earned XP
  yet.*, the Role Rewards tab reads *No role rewards configured.*, the
  Settings tab shows its heading with no controls under it, and the
  **System Status** card reads *Disabled* whatever your server is really
  set to. Read an empty tab as "not shown to me," never as "nothing is
  configured" — and check your permissions before acting on it. Changing
  something you lack the permission for fails with an *Insufficient
  permissions* message. While the Permission System panel reads *Inactive —
  all admins with MANAGE_GUILD have full access.*, every admin with Manage
  Server has full access to all five tabs.

## Using the leveling system

### How members earn XP from messages

Every message a member sends in your server earns them **XP per Message**
plus a random bonus of 0 to 9, which is then multiplied by any multipliers
that apply and rounded down. A message earns nothing when any of these is
true:

- **Enable Leveling** is off.
- The message was sent in a channel listed under **No-XP Channels**, or the
  member holds a role listed under **No-XP Roles**.
- Less time has passed than **XP Cooldown (seconds)** since the last message
  that earned that member XP. The cooldown is per member, not per channel.
- The message came from a bot, or from a direct message rather than a
  channel in your server.
- The message came from a channel in your server, but the bot had no
  membership details for its author attached to it. The message is skipped
  silently — exactly like an excluded one, with nothing said in Discord to
  mark the difference.

When a message pushes a member over a level boundary, the bot posts a
level-up announcement (if **Level-up Announcements** is on) and grants every
role reward at or below their new level.

### How members earn XP from voice

Counting starts when a member joins a voice channel while neither muted nor
deafened (by themselves or by the server) and at least two non-bot members
are in the channel, counting themselves. Those conditions are checked again
each time that member's own voice state changes: muting or deafening stops
the count, and unmuting starts a fresh one. Another member leaving does not
stop the count by itself — it is noticed the next time the counted member's
own voice state changes.

The XP lands when the count stops — when the member leaves the channel, or
when one of those conditions breaks — and is **Voice XP per Minute** times
the number of *whole* minutes counted. A stretch shorter than a minute earns
nothing. Voice XP ignores **No-XP Channels**, **No-XP Roles**, and every
multiplier; only **Enable Leveling** and **Voice XP** can switch it off.
A level-up reached through voice grants role rewards but never posts an
announcement.

### What a level costs

Reaching level 1 takes 100 XP, level 2 another 155, level 3 another 220,
and each level after that costs more than the one before it. `/rank` shows
progress inside the current level rather than the running total, so a member
with 300 XP altogether reads as level 2 with 45 of the 220 XP needed for
level 3.

### The slash commands

Three slash commands, run from a channel in your server. Each has a
per-member cooldown: after someone runs a command, that same command is
unavailable to them for the number of seconds listed, and running it early
replies with the remaining wait.

| Command | What it does | Permission required | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/rank` | Shows one member's level, XP, rank, message count, and voice time. | No permission gate is set in source — any member who can see the channel can run `/rank`. | 5s |
| `/leaderboard` | Shows the server's XP ranking, ten members at a time. | No permission gate is set in source — any member who can see the channel can run `/leaderboard`. | 5s |
| `/xp` | Sets, adds, or removes a member's XP. | Manage Server (`ManageGuild`) | 3s |

- `/rank` — **user** (optional; defaults to you). The reply shows level, XP
  earned inside the current level against what the level needs, a progress
  bar, rank out of the number of members holding a leveling record (the same
  count the dashboard's **Ranked Members** card shows — see below for what
  that includes), messages counted, and voice time. Rank **#N/A** means one
  thing only: this member has no leveling record at all, and they read level 0
  alongside it. A member who *has* a record but sits at 0 XP — someone taken
  there by `/xp set 0` or `/xp remove` — reads a numbered rank instead, because
  the rank counts the members ahead of them and adds one.
- `/leaderboard` — **page** (optional, 1 or higher; defaults to 1). Members
  are ordered by total XP, highest first, ten to a page, with medals on the
  top three and a footer giving the page number and how many members are
  ranked. Asking for a page past the end replies *No one has earned XP yet.
  Start chatting!* — the same reply an empty server gives.
- `/xp` has three subcommands, each taking **user** and **amount**:
  **set** (amount 0 or higher) writes that exact total; **add** (1 or
  higher) adds to it; **remove** (1 or higher) subtracts from it and stops
  at 0 rather than going negative. The reply is visible to everyone in the
  channel.

`/xp` recalculates the member's level from their new total and grants every
role reward at or below it. Three things it does not do: it never posts a
level-up announcement, it never takes a reward role back when the total
drops, and `/xp add` — unlike set and remove — also counts as a message for
that member, adding 1 to their message count and restarting their XP
cooldown, so their next real message earns nothing until the cooldown runs
out.

`/rank` and `/leaderboard` keep working while **Enable Leveling** is off.
They read the totals already recorded; switching leveling off stops new XP
from being earned, it does not hide or erase what members have.

### Where the settings live

Open **Leveling** from the guild sidebar. The page is headed *Leveling
System* and has three cards across the top — **Ranked Members**, **Role
Rewards**, and **System Status** — over five tabs: **Leaderboard**,
**Settings**, **Role Rewards**, **Exclusions**, and **Multipliers**.

### The three cards

**Ranked Members** counts the leveling records stored for this server, which
is not the same as the number of members who currently have XP. A record is
created the first time a member earns or is given XP, and nothing removes
it: `/xp set 0`, or `/xp remove` taking someone down to 0, leaves a record
at 0 XP that is still counted, and a member who leaves the server keeps
their record and stays counted. So this number only ever grows, and can sit
above the number of current members with XP to their name.
**Role Rewards** counts the rewards configured on the Role Rewards tab.
**System Status** reads *Enabled* or *Disabled* to match the Enable Leveling
switch — and, as noted in the Prerequisites, also reads *Disabled* whenever
the page could not load your settings.

### Leaderboard tab

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Table columns | Rank, User ID, Level, XP, Messages, Voice Time. Members are identified by Discord user ID, not by name. Rows are ordered by total XP, highest first. |
| **Previous** / **Next** | Move through the ranking ten members at a time. They appear only when there is more than one page. |

The table is read-only: there is no control here for changing a member's XP.
Use `/xp` in Discord for that, then reload the page.

### Settings tab

Switches and the destination dropdown save the moment you change them. The
three number boxes save when you click away from them. If a number is
rejected — blank, negative, or outside the range listed below — nothing is
saved, and the box goes on showing what you typed. It snaps back to the
stored value the next time the page re-reads your settings, which happens
when you reload the page and after any successful save that writes leveling
settings — not only on this tab, but also **Save Exclusions** on the
Exclusions tab and adding or removing a multiplier on the Multipliers tab.
Either way, a number on screen is not proof it was stored.

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Enable Leveling** | *Toggle the entire leveling system on or off.* Off stops all XP from messages and from voice. Existing totals, levels, and rewards are untouched, and `/rank` and `/leaderboard` keep answering. |
| **XP per Message** | 1 to 1000. The base XP a message earns, before the 0–9 random bonus and before multipliers. Raising it speeds leveling for everyone. |
| **XP Cooldown (seconds)** | 0 to 3600. The minimum gap between two XP-earning messages from the same member; messages sent inside the gap earn nothing. Raising it slows leveling; 0 means every single message earns. |
| **Voice XP** | *Grant XP for time spent in voice channels.* Off stops voice XP without affecting message XP. Switching it off also hides the Voice XP per Minute box. |
| **Voice XP per Minute** | 0 to 100, shown only while Voice XP is on. XP earned per whole minute counted in voice. Set to 0 and voice time earns nothing and is not added to the member's voice-time total either — the Voice Time column and `/rank`'s voice figure stop growing. |
| **Level-up Announcements** | *Announce when a member levels up.* Off stops the announcement; the level, the XP, and the role rewards still happen. Only message-driven level-ups are ever announced — a level reached in voice is silent either way. |
| **Announcement Destination** | Shown only while announcements are on. Two choices: **Same Channel** posts the announcement in the channel where the member's message landed; **Direct Message** sends it to the member privately, and if their direct messages are closed nothing is sent and nothing is reported to you. The hint under the dropdown mentions typing a channel ID into an input above it; there is no such input, and the dropdown offers only these two destinations. |
| **Announcement Message** | Shown only while announcements are on. 1 to 500 characters. `{user}` becomes a mention of the member, `{level}` their new level, and `{username}` the display name on their Discord account — the global one, or their username if they have set no display name, and never a nickname they hold in your server — only the first occurrence of each is replaced, so writing `{user}` twice leaves the second one as literal text. The variable browser below the box lists `{user}` and `{level}`. Whatever you write, the announcement can only ever mention the member who levelled up: it cannot ping `@everyone`, `@here`, or any role. The preview underneath shows how the message will read. |

### Role Rewards tab

*Automatically assign roles when members reach certain levels.*

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Table columns | Level, Role ID, and a remove button per row. |
| **Level** (in *Add Role Reward*) | 1 to 100. Anything outside that replies *Level must be between 1 and 100* and nothing is added. |
| **Role ID** (in *Add Role Reward*) | A picker over your server's roles. Leaving it empty replies *Role ID is required*. |
| **Add Reward** | Saves the pairing. A level and role pairing that already exists is refused with *A reward for this level and role already exists*; two different roles at the same level are allowed. |
| Remove (bin) | Asks for confirmation, then deletes the reward. Members who already hold the role keep it — deleting a reward stops future grants, it does not strip anyone. |

Rewards are cumulative and catch up: whenever a member's level is
recalculated, the bot adds every reward role at or below that level they do
not already have. A member who jumps from level 1 to level 10 through
`/xp add` receives all the rewards in between. Nothing is ever removed when
a member's level drops.

### Exclusions tab

*Channels and roles that do not earn XP.*

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **No-XP Channels (comma-separated IDs)** | Despite the label, this is a picker, not a text field. Messages in the channels you select earn no XP at all. It lists standard text channels only — announcement, forum, and voice channels cannot be selected here. |
| **No-XP Roles (comma-separated IDs)** | Also a picker. A member holding any of the selected roles earns no message XP anywhere in the server. |
| **Save Exclusions** | Applies both lists. Unlike the Settings tab, nothing on this tab takes effect until you press this button; leaving the page first discards your changes. |

Neither list affects voice XP. A member excluded by role still earns voice
XP, and a voice channel cannot be excluded from this tab at all.

### Multipliers tab

*Set XP multipliers per channel or role. A multiplier of 2 doubles the XP
earned.*

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Channel Multipliers** / **Role Multipliers** tables | Every multiplier you have set, listed by Channel ID or Role ID with its value shown as e.g. *2x*, each with a remove button that asks for confirmation before deleting. |
| **Type** (in *Add Multiplier*) | **Channel** or **Role**, which decides what the ID picker beside it offers. Channel lists standard text channels only. |
| **ID** (in *Add Multiplier*) | The channel or role the multiplier applies to. |
| **Multiplier** (in *Add Multiplier*) | Greater than 0 and at most 10. A value outside that range replies *Multiplier must be between 0 and 10* and nothing is added; an empty box replies *ID and multiplier value are required*. The box's arrows move in steps of 0.1, but the step is not a rule — a value between steps, such as 2.05, is accepted, stored, and applied to XP exactly as typed. |
| **Add Multiplier** | Saves it. Setting a multiplier for a channel or role that already has one replaces the old value. |

Multipliers stack across the two kinds but not within them: message XP is
multiplied by the channel's multiplier, then by the *highest* multiplier
among the member's roles that have one, then rounded down. A member with
three multiplied roles gets the best of the three, not their product.
Multipliers apply to message XP only — voice XP is never multiplied.

## Related features

**Permissions**, **Role Panels**, and **Moderation** each have their own entry
in the guild sidebar and their own page in the dashboard. Of the three, only
Moderation has a page in this guide — [Moderation](/guide/features/moderation).
Here is where each of them meets leveling.

- **Permissions** — creating dashboard roles and granting View Leaderboard,
  Manage Settings, and Manage Rewards, which is what the Prerequisites
  section above refers to. The same page carries a **Manage Users**
  permission for leveling; no control on the Leveling page uses it today, so
  granting or withholding it changes nothing you can reach from the
  dashboard. To change someone's XP, use `/xp` in Discord.
- **Moderation** — the other place a member's standing in your server is
  recorded. Moderation cases and XP are kept separately: banning or warning
  a member does not touch their XP, level, or reward roles, and removing
  their XP does not create a case.
- **Role Panels** — the other way FluxCore gives members roles. Reward roles
  are granted by level and are never taken away; nothing about them is
  self-service.
